Author

Gary Dearing

Author of Fingerprint of Reality: Science, Design, and the Truth Beyond Matter

Gary Dearing writes from a Christian perspective about questions of science, evidence, meaning, and faith. His first published book, Fingerprint of Reality, follows a cumulative investigation from the character of the universe toward questions of purpose, moral reality, human brokenness, and Jesus Christ.

Dearing graduated from Reed-Custer High School in 1981 and served four years in the United States Navy, including service aboard USS Carl Vinson (CVN-70). He develops Christian publishing and educational resources through Solomon Christian Publishing and the broader Solomon For Jesus ministry.
