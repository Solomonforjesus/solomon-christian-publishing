About the Author

Gary Dearing is a writer with a lifelong interest in the intersection of evidence, meaning, and belief. His work has been shaped by decades of conversations with scientists, engineers, educators, pastors, and ordinary people who take ideas seriously and refuse to accept shallow answers to deep questions.

Coming from a background outside academic theology, Dearing approaches faith the way an investigator approaches a case—by following evidence across disciplines, testing assumptions, and allowing conclusions to emerge rather than forcing them. His interests center on how distinct lines of inquiry converge upon questions of purpose and authorship.

Fingerprint of Reality: Science, Design, and the Truth Beyond Matter is his first book. It reflects both a personal journey and the conviction that honest investigation does not threaten truth but refines it.

He lives in the United States with his wife and family.
