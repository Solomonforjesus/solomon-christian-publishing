Acknowledgments

This book did not come together alone.

I am grateful to the many people who, knowingly or not, shaped its questions long before it shaped its answers—friends, colleagues, coworkers, and others willing to wrestle honestly with hard ideas without demanding easy conclusions.

To those who challenged me intellectually: thank you for pressing the questions beyond the boundaries of comfort. Clear thinking is a gift, even when it costs us our pride.

To those who shared their stories—of loss, doubt, grief, hope, and persistence—you entrusted me with something sacred. I tried to honor it with care.

To the pastors, teachers, and believers whose faith was steady without being loud, thank you for showing that conviction does not require spectacle.

To my wife, Kathy: your patience, prayers, and quiet faith carried more weight than you know. You believed long before I understood why belief mattered, and you waited without demanding that I arrive on your timetable. This book would not exist without you.

And finally, to God—who does not fear investigation, recoil from honest questions, or lose patience with those who take the long way around—thank You for meeting me in the evidence and then meeting me beyond it.
