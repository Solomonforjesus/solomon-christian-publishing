Dedication

To my wife and daughter,
who have watched me laugh and cry, write and delete,
and begin again more times than I can count.

Thank you for the hours, days, and months you gave me
to disappear into this work.
Your patience and love carried me through every draft.

To a few good men I work with,
who unknowingly helped inspire this book
simply by being themselves.

And to those whose work, knowledge, insight, and creativity
helped inform these pages and strengthened the journey
that brought this manuscript to completion.
