Afterword

The farther I went, the more I saw the same pattern in places I’d never thought to look together. By the time I finished, I was more convinced that the world we can study and understand bears the marks of its Creator.

Computational Note

We ran the quantum calculations on actual IBM quantum processors through the company’s cloud platform. A classical simulation can help us anticipate how a quantum system might behave. Running the calculation on hardware gave us measurements from the processor itself, with the limitations that come with real equipment, including noise, decoherence, and measurement uncertainty.

We wanted to see what would happen when we used a structured search to evaluate evidence we had already weighted. Those weights were judgments we supplied. The processor ran the calculation using those inputs. The runs yielded a consistent pattern, with enough repeatability for me to take the results seriously.

I included that work because it added weight to the larger case. My conclusion about intention rests on the investigation as a whole. I find intention the more convincing explanation for a universe whose laws we can describe mathematically and in which information and life are possible.
