Fingerprint of Reality

Science, Design, and the Truth Beyond Matter

Is reality the product of blind accident—or does it bear the marks of intention?

Fingerprint of Reality examines evidence drawn from cosmology, mathematics, language, biology, archaeology, quantum computation, morality, and human consciousness.

What begins as an inquiry into science and design becomes a search for meaning, truth, and the One who stands beyond creation.

Our Publishing Mission

Solomon Christian Publishing exists to carry thoughtful, carefully developed Christian work into the world. We seek to publish writing that treats serious questions honestly, defends truth without compromise, respects the reader, and ultimately points beyond human wisdom to Jesus Christ.
